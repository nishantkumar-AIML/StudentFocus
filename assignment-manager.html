Does the board work when my phone has no cellular reception? +

Yes. The task board runs completely offline on your device, making it ideal for study basements or flight mode.

What should I do if Android or Google Play Protect blocks the APK? +

If Android blocks the StudentFocus APK, follow these steps:

  1. Download the APK only from the official StudentFocus release: https://studentfocus-apk.vercel.app/.
  2. Ensure your device has at least 50 mb of free storage space.
  3. Open the Google Play Store.
  4. Tap your profile icon in the top-right corner.
  5. Select Play Protect.
  6. Tap the Settings icon.
  7. If Play Protect is blocking a verified APK, carefully review the warning before proceeding.
  8. Keep Play Protect enabled whenever possible.
  9. If you temporarily disable app scanning, turn it back on immediately after installation.
