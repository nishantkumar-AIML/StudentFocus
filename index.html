Troubleshooting Guide

Frequently Asked Questions & Help

Encountering an issue during installation or setup? Find short, practical solutions below.

What is StudentFocus?+

StudentFocus is an AI-powered student productivity and study management application for Android (Android 8.0+). It combines an on-device focus stopwatch, 24-hour study heatmap, time waste auditor, Kanban task board, local study notes, class timetables, attendance tracking, GPA/CGPA calculators, 21-day habit challenges, and dual-mode StudentOS AI.

Who is StudentFocus designed for?+

StudentFocus is designed for school, college, and university students, as well as students preparing for competitive or semester exams. It is built for scholars who want an integrated, distraction-free environment to organize assignments, schedules, attendance, and study routines with complete on-device privacy and zero cloud lock-in.

What can students do with StudentFocus?+

Students can:

  • Plan Studies: Use the Study Pace & Goal Planner to calculate achievable daily hours within a 24-hour budget.
  • Manage Assignments: Track coursework across four Kanban stages (Uncomplete, To Do, In Progress, Done).
  • Organize Class Schedules: Build Monday-to-Saturday weekly timetables with automated routine alarms.
  • Track Attendance: Monitor subject-wise attendance percentages to stay safely above 75% or 80% exam eligibility thresholds.
  • Calculate Grades: Accurately calculate credit-weighted Semester Subject GPA and cumulative degree CGPA.
  • Focus & Time Sessions: Run high-precision focus stopwatch blocks with ambient sounds and notification tray status.
  • Prepare for Exams: Practice with revision flashcard decks and monitor countdown targets to test dates.
  • Build Habits: Maintain 21-day habit streaks and audit 24-hour routines into useful, necessary, and wasted hours.
Does StudentFocus have an AI study assistant?+

Yes. StudentFocus features StudentOS AI with a dual-mode architecture:

  • Offline Mode (Default): An on-device assistant that executes local directives (@help, @task, @routine, @attendance, @cgpa, @date) without network access, querying your local SQLite database directly.
  • Online Mode (Optional): A conceptual academic tutor for difficult theoretical questions (e.g., "What is polymorphism in Java?"). Personal notes, schedules, and grades never leave your phone.
Can StudentFocus work offline?+

Yes. StudentFocus is an offline-first application. All core features—including the focus timer, notes, timetable, attendance tracker, flashcards, 21-day habits, financial ledger, and Offline StudentOS AI—operate 100% locally on your phone using local SQLite storage without internet access or account registration.

Does StudentFocus help with exam preparation?+

Yes. StudentFocus provides dedicated exam preparation tools including exam countdown targets, credit-weighted GPA/CGPA calculators with reappear status flags, revision flashcard decks for spaced active recall, and attendance margin tracking to protect exam eligibility.

What should I do if Android shows "App not installed"?+

This message typically occurs due to one of four common reasons:

  • Insufficient Storage Space: Check that your phone has at least 50 mb of free internal storage.
  • Incomplete Download: Delete the file and re-download over a stable Wi-Fi connection from https://studentfocus-apk.vercel.app/.
  • Existing Conflicting Version: If you previously installed a test build with a differing signing key, uninstall the older version before installing the release build.
  • Android Version Compatibility: Ensure your phone runs Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later.
What if the APK won't open or says "Cannot open file"?+

Some mobile browsers lack built-in package installers. Open your device's native Files or Downloads manager app, navigate to the Download folder, and tap the downloaded APK directly from there. Ensure the file name ends in .apk and has not been saved as .zip.

What if the app crashes after installation?+

Try the following diagnostic steps:

  1. Restart your phone to clear temporary OS memory locks.
  2. Verify your phone has sufficient internal storage space available.
  3. Clear application cache via Android Settings > Apps > StudentFocus > Storage > Clear Cache.
  4. If the crash persists, report the issue with your device model and Android version on our official GitHub Issues page.
